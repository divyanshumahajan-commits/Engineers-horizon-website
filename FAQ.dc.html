01Are all Engineers Horizon projects MahaRERA registered?+

Yes. Every ongoing project is registered with MahaRERA — EH Sky Tower (PR1260002501000), EH Towers (P52100079038), IOS Prime (P52100053819) and IOS Prime B1 (P52100079123). You can look them up on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.

02Why is the price "on request"?+

Prices depend on the floor, view and configuration, and offers change from time to time. Share your details and we’ll send an up-to-date cost sheet for the homes that suit you.

03Can I visit the sample flat?+

Yes. The sample flat is open every day from 10 AM to 7 PM. Book a slot online or WhatsApp us on +91 84840 50579 and we’ll be ready for you.

04Do you help with home loans?+

We work with leading banks and can help you with pre-approvals, documents and disbursement schedules.

05What is carpet area?+

Carpet area is the usable floor area inside your walls — the space you can actually furnish. As per RERA, prices are quoted on carpet area.

06When will I get possession?+

Each project’s possession date is stated on its MahaRERA certificate. We share regular construction updates so you always know where things stand.

07Can NRIs buy an Engineers Horizon home?+

Absolutely. See our NRI Corner for video tours, paperwork help and payment guidance.

08What happens after I book?+

You’ll get a booking confirmation, then the agreement for sale is registered. Your relationship manager guides you through every payment milestone until handover.
