OUR STORY

We started Engineers Horizon over sixteen years ago as a team of engineers who wanted to build the kind of homes we'd buy ourselves — solid, sensibly planned and fairly priced.

From our first project in Sus to EH Sky Tower in Bavdhan, the approach hasn't changed. We spend our effort where you'll feel it every day: structure, planning, light and finishes. We keep our promises small and specific, and then we keep them. Today, more than 1,000 families live in an Engineers Horizon home across Sus, Wakad, Tathawade and Bavdhan.
